Enterprise ERP & Portals

CampusSphere — Enterprise College & School ERP

Client: Higher Education Institute & Campus Network
The Problem (Hurdle)

The institution relied on fragmented legacy software, manual paperwork for student records, offline cash receipts, and physical exam grading. This resulted in severe administrative delays, lost student payment reconciliations, and zero parental visibility into student attendance.

The Solution (Architecture)

Engineered an integrated multi-tenant cloud ERP with role-based dashboards (Admin, Faculty, Student, Parent). Integrated automated UPI/Card fee payment gateways with instantaneous PDF receipts, SMS triggers for absentee alerts, and a dynamic hall ticket and GPA generator.

The Measurable Results

Completely eliminated 85% of manual administrative paperwork and collected 100% of semester tuition fees digitally within the first 48 hours of each academic term with zero reconciliation errors.

100% Digital Collection
12,000+ Active Records
React 19 Node.js PostgreSQL Docker Razorpay / Stripe

Full-Stack SaaS Platform

PulseMetrics — Real-Time Cloud SaaS Analytics

Client: Venture-backed FinTech Startup (San Francisco, CA)
The Problem (Hurdle)

The client needed an observability and financial tracking dashboard capable of ingesting and rendering hundreds of live server and revenue events per second with sub-50ms query latency, zero browser stuttering, and strict tenant isolation.

The Solution (Architecture)

Engineered a reactive dashboard driven by binary WebSockets, optimized TimescaleDB time-series partitions, and client-side virtualized lists. Integrated Stripe Billing customer portal with automated tier upgrade webhooks and automated PDF financial summaries.

The Measurable Results

Delivered the MVP 2 weeks ahead of target schedule. Successfully supported over 2,500+ active business customers and helped the startup secure their $1.8M Seed financing round.

99.98% Uptime SLA
<45ms Query Latency
+340% MRR Growth
React 19 Node.js TimescaleDB Redis Pub/Sub Stripe Billing

Headless E-Commerce

Aurum — Luxury Audio & Gear Store

Client: Aurum Sound Labs (London, UK)
The Problem (Hurdle)

Their previous WooCommerce storefront suffered from a sluggish 4.5s initial load time, layout jumping on mobile, high cart abandonment, and poor visual presentation that failed to convey luxury product prestige.

The Solution (Architecture)

Rebuilt the entire storefront as a headless Next.js application with edge caching, optimistic slide-out cart drawer, and interactive 3D product previews. Integrated Shopify Storefront GraphQL API with Stripe Elements 1-click checkout.

The Measurable Results

Boosted gross monthly revenue by 62% in the first quarter post-launch, dropped cart abandonment by 28%, and achieved a stellar 99/100 Google Lighthouse performance rating.

AI Agents & Workflows

Synapse AI — Visual Agent Builder & Workflow Automation

Client: AutomateHQ (Austin, TX)
The Problem (Hurdle)

Operations teams spent over 12 hours weekly performing manual data extraction from PDFs and routing emails into CRM databases, lacking a no-code visual canvas to connect LLMs with internal databases securely.

The Solution (Architecture)

Architected an interactive drag-and-drop React Flow node canvas wired to an asynchronous Python FastAPI worker pool. Users connect custom prompts, vector stores (Pinecone/ChromaDB), and webhooks with instant test execution and token cost controls.

The Measurable Results

Over 1.2M automated workflow executions processed within 90 days of launch; featured on Product Hunt #2 Product of the Day and achieved a 4.9/5 user satisfaction score.
